About LaunchPad

Designed for teams that ship under pressure.

LaunchPad replaces scattered launch documents with a single operational system for brief, build, approve, publish, and review.

Operating model

One plan from first brief to final report.

Every route in this template includes import-friendly content patterns so the CLI can test schema inference, draft story generation, asset mapping, links, and fallback rendering.

1

Brief

Collect campaign goals, markets, channel requirements, and required approvals.

2

Coordinate

Assign owners, review assets, lock dependencies, and monitor launch readiness.

3

Report

Record shipped versions, final approvals, blockers, and post-launch outcomes.
